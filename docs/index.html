Selected Research Project

Implementation-focused work related to my research interests.

SUMMER: Recursive Zero-Knowledge Proofs for Scalable RNN Training

SUMMER is an end-to-end zero-knowledge proof of training system for recurrent neural networks. It combines verification for linear layers and non-linear activations with recursive proof composition to keep proof size and verification time independent of the number of training iterations.
